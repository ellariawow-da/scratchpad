Recommendations

execution only — no roster/strat changes
  1. 1
    Death recovery is the #1 lever (6:09 across 24 deaths)Every death costs twice — the rez itself, then the raid waiting at partial strength. Combat-rez priority to the highest-DPS warrior first; rebuff on the move (drive-by greater blessings/renews) instead of a full stop; pre-assign 'if you die on this boss, X rezzes you' before the pull. Targets: 4HM exit (0:27 + 0:38) and Gluth exit (1:14) alone are ~1:50; under 0:20 recovery after a bloody boss saves ~1:30 by itself.
  2. 2
    Pre-boss setup: 3:23 of dead time across 15 boss pulls7:02 counting gaps that also follow deaths. The 0:57 before Loatheb had zero deaths — pure staging. Lock assignments (spore groups, marks, positions) before the last trash pack dies and pull on a fixed countdown. Tanks take boss positions while the last pack is in execute range, not after it dies.
  3. 3
    Kill the 2:42 false startThe WCL timer starts at first combat, not first damage. Nobody touches anything until the raid is buffed, in position, and ready — and nobody scouts ahead of the group (Nuu, 18:30). This one is 2:42 of the 4:24 PB gap, available for free.
  4. 4
    Transitions: 1:55 in ≥5s gaps plus 1:00 of micro-gaps27 of 47 pulls started after a ≥5s gap (7.8s average). Move during the last ~10% of each pack: melee peel toward the next pack as mobs hit execute range, ranged/healers pre-position, drink while walking. Halving the average transition saves ~1:45 over a run.
  5. 5
    Within-pull trash paceRaid DPS swings from 8.7k (Spider Pull 8, a 1:41 trickle) to 49k on clean packs. On any pack that will live past ~15s, the next pack should already be arriving — chain-pull instead of watching the tail die.
  6. 6
    Boss time is near ceiling except the add phasesGothik 4:58 and KT 5:32 are add throughput, not boss DPS: apply the blender rules (no sunders on <5s adds, execute the add that gates the phase). Idle is 31.5% of the run and the PB gap is only 4:24 — this is where the next PB lives.

Calls on the pull data

what the 47 pulls say
Spider Wing Pull 8 (18:36, 9 mobs, 676k HP) is the worst pull of the run: 1:41 at 8.7k raid DPS. A trickle gauntlet (Plagued Ghouls + Dread Creepers) where mobs kept arriving instead of being pulled together — Flaps died during it. Combined with the clean 47k pulls around it, this single pull is worth ~30–40s if it's pulled as one pack.
Construct Wing Pulls 3–4 overlap (19:02): 3 Embalming Slimes took 45s at 833 raid DPS while the next 33-mob pack was already engaged at 19:02:39 — the raid outran its own mop-up and left slimes to a skeleton crew. Fine if intentional; if not, execute-range stragglers need a finisher call.
Plague Wing Pull 7 (45 mobs, 642k HP, 39s, 21.3k raid DPS) — the bat/grub swarm is the biggest pull of the run and still only mid-pack DPS; AoE classes are under-represented in damage here (see the class table below).
Best pulls: Plague Wing Pulls 4–6 (Stoneskin Gargoyle pairs at 61–69k raid DPS) and Military Wing Pull 10 (15 mobs, 70.5k) show the raid's ceiling — stacked packs, everyone in range at pull start.
Gap discipline: worst pre-pull gaps are recovery, not travel — Construct Pull 1 (65s, after the 7-man 4HM death bill) and Construct Pull 10 (53s, Patchwerk kill → Grobbulus door). Only four other pulls waited >15s all run.

The break-even

first Sunder stack
0.03 × D × L > 1,000

Cost: one 1.5s GCD, worth roughly one Bloodthirst or about 1,000 damage. Benefit: one Sunder removes 450 armor, worth approximately 3% more physical damage taken. Here D is physical DPS hitting the mob and L is the mob’s remaining lifetime.

6 warriors: the first stack needs about 9.5s of time-to-die to pay back.

The model

warriors only · deliberately conservative
WW-only kill time = pack HP / (DPS wars × min(4, mobs) × 744 / 10s)
  • Whirlwind cooldown 10s, hits at most 4 targets (Classic Era cap).
  • DPS wars = the 18 non-tank warriors (excludes MT Vincint and offtanks Bootythweat, Sven, Sempai, Tankina, Egg); the war count per pack is how many actually damaged that pack in the log.
  • Pack HP = sum of the mobs' max HP, read from the combat log's advanced unit block.
  • Warriors alone, Whirlwind only. A real no-Sunder opener also adds Bloodthirst, Mortal Strike, executes, auto-attacks and every other class's damage on top. If Whirlwind alone matches the actual kill time, Sunders on that pack bought nothing.
  • A pack only earns a verdict if all its mobs were engaged within an 8-second span and at least 6 DPS warriors damaged it. Wider packs are chain/trickle segments (mobs arriving over time) and thin packs were killed by a subset; both are listed separately with no verdict.

Methodology and caveats

how the numbers were built

Mob set

650 real trash mobs with a measured time-to-die (the Blender tabs' 674-mob universe additionally counts 18 boss/unit GUIDs and Web Wraps). Critters and target dummies excluded; packs made of mobs first damaged inside boss encounter windows are flagged as boss-encounter packs, not trash packs.

Packs

Mobs clustered by first-damage time with a 6-second gap, the same segmentation as the Warrior Rotation and Blender tabs. Pack duration is first damage to last death.

Mob HP

Read from the combat log itself: in spell/ranged damage events, fields[15] is the target's max HP (the advanced unit block), verified constant per species across the whole log (Soldier of the Frozen Wastes 2,000; Spectral Trainee 5,030; Embalming Slime 12,208; Dread Creeper 47,160; Crypt Guard 204,360). The rare mob with no spell damage event falls back to player damage taken minus the killing blow's overkill.

The retired HP bug

fields[31] is not overkill (it is never 0 and exceeds the hit on 43.5% of creature damage events), so nothing here subtracts it per-hit. This is also what the mob-HP fields above rely on instead.

WW pricing

Observational across all 24 warriors' gear and buffs; the 1-stack dip (see the stack table) is target mix, not an argument against Sunder.

Model limits

The model ignores rage: it assumes every DPS warrior can Whirlwind on cooldown from the pull start. It also ignores tank Whirlwinds, so real no-Sunder kill times would be faster than the model's. Verdicts use only packs of 3+ mobs; 1-2 mob leftovers are in the data file but not headlined.

IV Score

Our own warrior ranking, built to fix the gap between PMS’s model and the blender analysis. PMS values every effective sunder at a flat 7,000 damage — a figure derived from a ~100k-HP mob — while our data shows half of trash dies in under 5s. IV Score weights each sunder by how long its target actually lived.

IV Score v1

How IV Score works

sunder value follows target lifetime
Score = 10 × (Damage + SunderValue + SoftPoints − Waste − TSA) / 3,600,000

The 3.6M “10-point” yardstick is PMS’s own for this raid, so IV Score and PMS are directly comparable — same scale, different treatment of the sunder.

<3s0 per sunder · blender range
3–5s1,400 per sunder
5–10s3,500 per sunder
10s+7,000 per sunder · PMS parity

Each effective sunder — a cast made while its target had fewer than five sunder stacks — is weighted by that target’s time-to-die, measured from first damage to death. Targets that never died during the log count as 10s+.

Waste

−500 per sunder cast at the five-stack cap — a GCD that could have been a roughly 1,000-damage Bloodthirst. Caveat: for the main tank, refreshing at five stacks is part of holding threat, so read tank rows with care.

Soft points

PMS’s values and caps, unchanged: Demo Shout 3,500 (cap 4), Disarm 14,000 (cap 4), successful interrupt 28,000 (cap 6), Challenging Shout 21,000 (cap 2), Dummies 14,000 per point (Masterwork = 1, Advanced/Love Fool = 0.5, cap 5), and 2× damage from Sapper, Dynamite, Holy Water, and Chili.

TSA penalty

−100,000 for flagged recipients: Dinkie, Jeian, and Iynx, per PMS’s flags on this run. Provisional until the flags are confirmed.

Damage

The warrior’s total damage from the raw combat log (Ellaria POV), which can differ slightly from Warcraft Logs. Roles from the Angry Era assignments: MT Vincint; OT Bootythweat, Sven, Sempai, Tankina, and Egg.

Methods + Sources

What each number means, what the log can see, and where the source reports live.

Effective Sunder

A Sunder cast while the target had fewer than five stacks. This matches the PMS convention and counts casts, not landed applications.

Targeted opener

The first targeted on-GCD cast on each mob. Incidental Whirlwind hits are excluded, so the choice is usually Sunder or Bloodthirst.

Pull-start opener

The first attacking GCD cast by each warrior in a pull window. Whirlwind counts here because this view asks which button the warrior pressed first, not which mob it hit.

Arrival rank

Each warrior’s first targeted GCD timestamp is ranked within each mob: 0.00 is first and 1.00 is last. Pull delay is measured from the first warrior’s attacking GCD.

Pull segmentation

Trash mobs clustered by first player-damage time with a six-second gap. The 58 clusters—and 37 with four or more mobs—are heuristic, not a route map.

Capture limits

Ellaria’s melee POV gives near-complete melee coverage. Mage and hunter damage can be missing beyond ~40 yards, though nearby casts remain visible.

Damage parsing

Advanced log build 1.15.9: spell/range amount is payload field 29; swing amount is field 26. Duplicate SWING_DAMAGE_LANDED rows were excluded.

Timing

The PB comparison and high-level split use WCL fight-entry durations: 19:35 vs 19:01. The raw log’s first-damage-to-kill pass resolves Sep 29 to ~19:26 and is used only for event-level timing.
