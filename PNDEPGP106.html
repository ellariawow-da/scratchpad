5 Second Blender

314 warrior-engaged trash mobs. Break-even rule from Sep 29 still holds (a GCD ≈ 1k BT; ~+3% phys per stack): mobs dying in <5s don't repay a Sunder; 10s+ mobs do. Oct 6's twist: DPS sunder-first is flat by time-to-die — habit, not selection — but the bands themselves behave differently because chain-pulled trash means TTD (from first raid damage) often predates a warrior's engagement.
