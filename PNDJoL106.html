Judgement of Light on trash: where it pays, where it’s wasted

JoL is a free 81 HP heal on every melee hit against the judged target — but only if melee are actually hurt and the target lives long enough. On Oct 6 it returned 79,756 effective healing on trash from just 34 applications, almost all of it from a handful of packs. The rest of the instance shows the two failure modes clearly: judging mobs that die in seconds, and never judging the long Construct pulls at all.

The one-sentence answer

Judge gargoyles, slimes, belchers, golems and scientist packs in the first second — and stop judging everything else. Gargoyle pairs alone returned 29,081 effective in ~20 seconds across two pulls. Construct scientist/slime pulls of the same shape returned zero, because they were never judged.

Bosses are kept in their own section at the bottom (§ Bosses). Patchwerk is not a miss — see the callout there.

Verdict: three patterns explain almost everything

Pays

Tanky pair + full melee stack + real damage

Every top pack shares the shape: 1–3 mobs that live 7–15s, the whole melee pile on the judged target, and incoming damage that leaves people below full HP so procs land. Gargoyles are the prototype; Sewage Slimes and Sludge Belchers splash melee the same way (5–22% overheal on Construct pulls).

Wasted

Late, or onto a dying mob

Every wasted judgement was applied at +3.4s or later, or onto a mob with under ~5s left to live. 17 applications all night landed on mobs that died in <5s. Necropolis Acolytes: 5 applications, average aura 1.0s. The GCD and re-seal buy almost nothing.

Missed

Long pulls never judged at all

The biggest upside isn’t fixing the waste — it’s the Construct Wing. Scientist/slime Pulls 5, 6, 11 and 13 (16–37s each) all went unjudged while judged Construct pulls in the same wing returned at 5–22% overheal. Same mobs, same melee damage — just no judgement.

The 8-second rule the data supports: if the target won’t live 8s+, or the pull won’t last 10s+, don’t spend the GCD. If it will — judge it in the first 2 seconds, on the longest-lived mob, and don’t chase when it dies.

✕ Wasted — a GCD for almost nothing

Two distinct wastes: chasing (re-judging onto trickle mobs mid-pull) and short-pull judging (spending the opener GCD on packs that die in 3–7s).

Chasing: Plague 3 & Plague 8

Plague 3 (29.2s) — 4 applications, 2,020 effective, 88.6% overheal. First ghoul judged at +3.2s died 2.0s later; re-judges at +13.2 / +15.1 / +23.2 chased trickle mobs while the raid sat topped off. Coverage read 61.6% and it still returned nothing — coverage on the wrong mobs doesn’t count.

Plague 8 (44.2s, the longest real trash pull) — only 17.8% covered, 2,141 effective. All 3 applications landed on dying adds: a Plague Beast with 3.9s to live, Mutated Grubs with 1–3s. The long-lived target was never judged. A 44-second pull is exactly where JoL should shine.

The playbook — how to adjust

Five rules, each traceable to a pull above. Nothing here changes assignments or routing — it’s purely which target gets the judgement GCD, and when.

Judge by mob type, not by wing

Always: Stoneskin Gargoyles, slimes (Sewage / Embalming), Sludge Belchers, Patchwork Golems, scientist packs (Mad Scientist / Surgical Assistant). Skip: Deathknight / Shade / Unholy-weapon packs and anything dying in <8s — the Military short-pull rows show why.

Split the pair — both paladins, first second

On two-tanky-mob pulls, each judge takes one mob inside the first second. That’s the Plague 6/7 pattern: 14k+ effective in ~10s, twice. A single judgement on one gargoyle leaves the other half of the melee pile unhealed.

Judge the longest-lived mob, once — no chasing

When the judged mob dies, re-judge only if a 10s+ target remains and melee are actually hurt. Plague 3 (4 apps, 88.6% overheal) and Plague 8 (3 apps, all on 1–4s adds) are the anti-examples.

Add the Construct scientist pulls, Spider 1 and Plague 1

Construct 5 / 6 / 11 / 13 join the judged list — same shapes that already paid on Construct 1 / 9 / 15. Spider 1 (17.4s, 80k melee taken) and Plague 1 golems (52.8k) with them. Leave Military short pulls alone.

First GCD matters more than coverage %

Every best pack was judged at +0 to +2.6s; every wasted one at +3.4s or later, or onto a dying add. If the judgement can’t be in the first two seconds on the right mob, it’s usually better not to throw it at all.

Bosses — kept separate

Boss judging is a different question from trash, so it lives down here on its own. Headline: boss coverage is genuinely good — 84.5% on judgeable targets — and two fights carry the night.

Patchwerk: 0% JoL — and that is correct. Do not judge him.

Hateful Strike targets the highest-HP non-tank, so melee DPS intentionally keep their HP low to keep strikes landing on the designated soakers. Judgement of Light would heal melee up into Hateful range — the “free heal” would actively break the strat. Patchwerk is therefore excluded from boss coverage in the score, as a deliberate non-use — the same category as skipping a sub-8s trash mob, not a miss. An earlier draft of this analysis flagged Patchwerk as the biggest gap; that was wrong and is retracted here.

Sapphiron — JoL’s showcase

105,234 effective · 1,551 procs · 99.5% uptime · 16.2% overheal. Constant frost-aura damage means nearly every proc lands on someone actually missing health. One fight is a fifth of JoL’s entire night.

Loatheb — procs bypass the lockout

61,450 effective · 2,455 procs · 96.3% uptime (Nuu). JoL procs aren’t cast heals, so they flow straight through Loatheb’s healing lockout. Nuu’s refresh discipline — 91.2 of 94.7 seconds — is the model for long fights.

Methods & definitions

How this was measured — tap to expand
  • The log spells it “Judgement” — aura spell 20346; heal proc spell 20343, 81 HP per proc at rank 4. Heal amount = log field 29, overheal = field 31 (calibrated: overheal is 0 at low HP, equals amount at full HP).
  • Applications / refreshes / removals from SPELL_AURA_APPLIED / REFRESH / REMOVED for Judgement of Light, per target. Coverage on a pull = union of JoL aura time on any mob in the window ÷ pull duration.
  • Pull windows are the run’s counted pulls (6s-inflow definition). Construct Pull 14 — the span containing the Grobbulus wipe, hearth-out and re-entry — is excluded from all trash stats.
  • Damage taken per pull is summed from damage events with player destinations inside the pull window; “melee taken” restricts to melee/tank recipients.
  • Trash totals (79,756 effective / 148,717 gross / 53.6% efficiency) and both scores are the dashboard brief’s headline figures; per-pull rows come from the same analyzers (analyze_jol_oct6.py, analyze_jol_trash_oct6.py). Log POV: Junsa. Character names only.
  • Patchwerk and Gothik are excluded from boss coverage by design: Patchwerk for the Hateful Strike / low-HP melee strat, Gothik because the boss target isn’t hittable for most of the window. Kel’Thuzad is counted from boss activation.
